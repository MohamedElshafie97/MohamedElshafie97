## Mohamed Elshafie

Senior Systems Engineer at **Axis Pay** (fintech), Cairo.

I look after the infrastructure that keeps a payments business running: Linux and Windows servers, virtualization, storage, backups, and the databases sitting on top of them. Most of my week goes into making things boring in the good sense: predictable patching, tested restores, replication that recovers on its own, and automation for anything I've had to do by hand more than twice.

### What I work on

- **Virtualization** – VMware vSphere/ESXi, Proxmox, and an ongoing evaluation of XCP-ng + Xen Orchestra as a vCenter replacement (migration testing, HA, backup integration)
- **Databases** – MariaDB/MySQL replication with GTID, including a DR replica in a second site; day-to-day SQL Server administration
- **Storage & backup** – Dell EMC Unity, Dell EMC Data Domain and QNAP; backup targets, retention policies, and restore verification across two sites (HQ and a secondary branch)
- **Hardware** – Dell PowerEdge and HPE ProLiant servers
- **Linux** – RHEL/Rocky and Ubuntu/Debian; hardening, patching, service troubleshooting
- **Windows** – Active Directory, Group Policy, Windows Server administration
- **Automation** – Ansible, Bash, PowerShell
- **Containers** – Docker and Docker Compose
- **Monitoring** – Prometheus (federated across dev, staging and production), Alertmanager, Grafana
- **Cloud** – Azure (IaaS, identity, hybrid with on-prem AD)

### Currently learning

Kubernetes administration (working toward the CKA). I've worked on Kubernetes monitoring alongside our DevOps team and now I'm going deeper on running clusters myself. Also CI/CD with GitHub Actions, in a home lab for now.
